Alerts

Two feeds. Sensor alerts are what the alert engine hears from IMD, CWC, NCS, GSI and the satellite EO pass — they reach the backend over the agencies' own national telemetry, so they keep arriving when the sector is dark. Responder alerts are what the field raises (SOS, backup) or what the platform infers about it (unreachable, low battery, overdue check-in, entered a critical cell, broadcast not acknowledged).
